# Product cut-outs (images without background)

The main image of each seeded product is shown without a background so product cards look clean and even.

* Most cut-outs were made from the product photos in `../photos` (Open Images, CC BY 2.0) by removing the
  background automatically (rembg, U2-Net / IS-Net and Segment Anything models), cropping to the product and
  placing it on a transparent 640 x 640 WebP canvas. **These are changed versions of the original photos**; the
  authors and original links are listed in `../photos/CREDITS.md`, and the unchanged photo is the first
  picture of each product's gallery.
* Where a photo was a close-up that could not be cut out cleanly, the cut-out is the matching 3D illustration
  from Microsoft Fluent Emoji (MIT licence), the same set used in `../seed`:
